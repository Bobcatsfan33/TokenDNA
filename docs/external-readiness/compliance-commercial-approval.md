# Compliance, Privacy, Legal, and Commercial Approval

Owner: security governance, privacy, legal, and product leadership

Select the actual target framework and deployment boundary; repository OSCAL,
STIG, or control-mapping artifacts are engineering inputs, not an authorization.

Required evidence:

- approved system boundary, data-flow diagram, data classification, and threat model;
- privacy assessment, retention/deletion rules, and data-processing terms;
- completed customer/vendor responsibility matrix;
- framework-specific control assessment and POA&M;
- licensing, support, export, accessibility, and incident-notification review;
- named authorizing executive and dated release decision.

Acceptance: every applicable authority signs the release boundary and residual
risk record; no document may claim FedRAMP, DoD ATO, or certification without
the corresponding external authorization.
